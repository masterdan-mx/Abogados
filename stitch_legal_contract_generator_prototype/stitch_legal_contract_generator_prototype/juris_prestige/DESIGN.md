## Brand & Style

The design system projects authority, legal rigor, and modern operational efficiency. Designed specifically for enterprise legal practices, general counsels, and high-tier law firms, the visual language bridges historic prestige with high-velocity legal automation.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Corporate Elegance**: A tailored union between classical legal authority and contemporary SaaS ergonomics. It foregoes ephemeral software trends in favor of disciplined proportions, architectural white space, and balanced framing.
- **Architectural Structure**: Clean horizontal registers, deliberate vertical grid alignments, and restrained framing evoke classical courthouses and modern law offices.
- **Tone & Mood**: Trustworthy, precise, institutional yet accessible. The interface inspires total confidence through pixel-perfect alignment, rich midnight depths, and warm, refined brass accents.

## Colors

The color palette anchors the legal workspace in deep, commanding dark navies while using measured gold highlights for focus, state changes, and premium affordance.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0f2027` & `#1b2a4a`)**: The structural anchor. Used for primary command elements, solid action buttons, sidebars, executive headers, and critical interactive surfaces.
- **Refined Gold (`#c59b27` with `#d4af37` highlight)**: Reserved for accents, selected states, high-priority validation highlights, and warm secondary actions. Gold must never dominate the canvas; it operates as an authoritative seal or foil stamp.
- **Porcelain & Crisp Neutrals (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`)**: Form the clean backdrop of generated legal drafts, sheet containers, and workspace canvasses, providing optimal document contrast.
- **Charcoal Text Hierarchy (`#1e293b`, `#334155`, `#64748b`)**: High legibility without the harshness of pure black. Ideal for lengthy clauses, contract previews, and metadata tags.
- **Border Neutral (`#e2e8f0`)**: Subtle structure line defining card sections, form input perimeters, and split-screen workbench panels.

## Typography

The typographical pairing relies on the heritage and editorial prestige of **Playfair Display** combined with the contemporary clarity and high legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Headings & Key Titles (Playfair Display)**: Infuses pages with the gravitas of traditional legal publications, judicial chambers, and distinguished corporate identity.
- **Workflows & Contractual Data (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures instant clarity for multi-column intake fields, contract clauses, tabular disclosures, and micro-labels.
- **Label Case**: Form and section headers utilize uppercase tracked labels (`label-md` and `label-sm`) with subtle tracking (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) to structure complex legal questionnaires.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a dual-pane workbench paradigm alongside a disciplined 12-column responsive layout. 

### Grid & Workbench Model
- **Split-Screen Workspace**: Desktop views default to an asynchronous dual-pane layout: a left-side data collection flow (5 columns) paired with a live, real-time legal document preview canvas (7 columns).
- **Column Rhythm**: 12-column layout on desktop with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `2rem` page margins.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: Dual-pane simultaneous generation and verification.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Responsive stacked or tabbed preview mode with `1.25rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single column flow; inputs collapse to vertical orientation; document preview moves into a modal sheet or secondary step.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained using layered porcelain surfaces, architectural micro-borders, and subtle ambient drop shadows that simulate stacked parchment and high-grade office stationery.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#f8fafc`)**: Neutral, non-reflective ground plane.
- **Panel / Card Tier (`#ffffff`)**: Clean white surface with a hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) and subtle ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(15, 32, 39, 0.04)`).
- **Document Sheet Tier**: Contract paper surfaces use elevated ambient shadows (`0 8px 30px rgba(15, 32, 39, 0.08)`) with crisp internal margin padding to replicate standard legal paper sheets.
- **Modal / Floating Actions**: Elevated overlay shadow (`0 16px 40px rgba(15, 32, 39, 0.14)`) combined with a fine border (`1px solid #c59b27` at 30% opacity).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, measured corner radius (`roundedness: 2` / base `0.5rem` or `8px`), offering a clean transition between structural corporate discipline and friendly ergonomics.

### Shape Guidelines
- **Input Fields & Buttons**: Standard `0.5rem` (8px) radius conveys dependability without feeling casual or toy-like.
- **Cards & Document Sheets**: `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px to 16px) corner radius.
- **Pills / Status Chips**: Fully rounded borders (`9999px`) reserved only for document execution badges (e.g., "Drafting", "Pending Review", "Executed").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Navy background (`#0f2027`), crisp white text, subtle gold highlight border (`1px solid #c59b27`), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, corner radius `0.5rem`. Subtle warm glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Action**: Rich navy outline (`1.5px solid #1b2a4a`) or gold rim, with navy text, transparent background, and `0.5rem` radius.
- **Icon / Utility**: Compact button rows for file exports (e.g., "Generar PDF", "Enviar Mail") featuring primary navy ground, gold border perimeter, and centered SVG icons.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Perimeter & Field**: White surface, charcoal input text (`#1e293b`), wrapped in a crisp border (`1.5px solid #cbd5e1`).
- **Focus State**: Deep navy border with an outer ring in muted gold (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`).
- **Labels**: Strict uppercase styling, bold weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans 11px`, tracking `0.05em`), charcoal color (`#334155`), placed directly above fields with `0.375rem` spacing.

### Legal Document Preview Card
- **Parchment Surface**: Pure white canvas styled with standard A4 / Letter visual proportions, bounded by a fine outline (`#e2e8f0`) and elevated shadow.
- **Watermark & Seals**: Subtle background seal rendered in low-opacity navy/gold for active drafts.
- **Contract Clauses**: Structured with Playfair Display subsection headers and crisp Plus Jakarta Sans legal text, featuring formal indented clause definitions.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Frame**: `18px x 18px` square with `4px` radius; checked state adopts `#0f2027` with a gold checkmark glyph.

### Status Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Style**: Pill-shaped badges (`rounded-full`), `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding, utilizing low-saturation tint backgrounds (e.g., gold-tinted `#fef9c3` with `#854d0e` text, or navy `#e0f2fe` with `#0369a1` text).
